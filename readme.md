# Sin Star: Book One

**By Louiery R. Sincioco (Sin)**

Arin wants a place to belong. When his life becomes entangled with the Axiom—a
power capable of reshaping reality—his search for connection draws him into a
conflict that reaches far beyond the worlds he knows.

Alongside companions who become his chosen family, Arin must decide what strength
is for and what it means to protect another person. Across that journey stands
Kael, whose grief and extraordinary power have turned the hope of ending
suffering into a struggle over freedom, control, and the possibility of redemption.

*Sin Star* is a science-fantasy story about friendship, belonging, responsibility,
and finding reasons to care for an imperfect universe.

**[Read and listen to Book One](https://sincioco.github.io/SinStar_Audio_BookOne/)**

The illustrated reader includes synchronized narration, passage highlighting,
chapter navigation, playback controls, and optional offline audio downloads.
Follow is enabled whenever the reader opens and stays enabled until its checkbox
is explicitly unchecked. Offline storage and saved progress depend on the browser
and the address used to open the reader.

## Updating this site

GitHub Pages publishes `docs/` from `main`. That folder is the public deployment
copy; the authoritative novel and original audio remain in the separate
[SinStarI project](https://github.com/Sincioco/SinStarI).

The local `Sincioco.com/tools/Update-BookOne.ps1` workflow supports `-DryRun`,
update, `-Check`, and recovery. It validates the explicit public-file list and
audio hashes before copying. Review changes, then commit and push separately.
The companion `README-BookOne.md` beside that local script documents the workflow.
Do not add credentials, authoring tools, or original audio masters to this repo.
